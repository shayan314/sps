About Our School

A legacy of three decades in nurturing young minds with knowledge, discipline, and values.

Why Choose SPS?

SPS School is affiliated with the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (BISE), Lahore. Our curriculum is designed to meet national standards while fostering critical thinking and creativity.

Our experienced faculty, modern facilities, and supportive environment ensure every student reaches their full potential. We believe in producing not just good students, but good citizens.

With separate campuses for boys and girls, well-equipped science and computer labs, a vast library, and excellent sports facilities — SPS provides a complete educational experience.
